failed campaign and fixture-debug journals remain closed, read-only parents.
The frozen learner85e77bde, preparation62ef1897, baselines75004394, model4d53c2fb,
converter7e0af843 and scientific26-world plan0187415c remain byte-identical.

`binding_transfer_v2.py` scopes four bindings: the learner's accounting factory,
the preparation module's accounting object, its explicit ledger argument, and
the learner's stage-metadata entrypoint. Baselines share the exact same learner

strict science/route hash checks; existing workers close failed startup normally.

learner preparation function into `binding-transfer-v2/learner-prepared-v1.json`
and saves a separate `prelabel-routing-v1.json` pinning the wrapper, new accounting,
frozen sources and scientific plan. Both files belong in the new frozen config.
