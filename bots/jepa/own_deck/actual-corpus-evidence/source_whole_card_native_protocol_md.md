# Actual-card embeddings before deck composition

Preparation only. The old six literal forwards and the artificial two-card bag
experiment are held. No source in this protocol authorizes a native or neural
launch. Sources, native fixtures, data, objective and guards must be frozen
before the coordinator's GO; no selection from held results is permitted.

The actual 27 declarations contain 251 main-card identities and 1,432 copies.
Runtime admission covers 115 identities/635 copies, not measured behavior.
The previous twelve action vectors cover zero main copies. This pilot instead
uses eight actual cards covering 65 copies (4.54%): ASH_048, JTL_095, SEC_163,
LOF_077, JTL_078, JTL_158, LOF_155 and JTL_217. Runtime admission, complete
frontend representation, measured fixture paths, pretraining and semantic
qualification remain separate flags. No completion of fitting sets a semantic
validation flag. No claim about all 115 admitted cards follows from this pilot.

## Complete reviewed input

`whole_card_encoder.py` reads full pinned catalog rows. It serializes main-card
role; printed types, cost, uniqueness, arena, stats and upgrade bonuses; aspects,
traits and keywords; numeric keywordValues; root attachCondition; and every
ordered ability side/index with its trigger, costs, selectors, conditions,
effects and continuations. Genuine rule strings/references remain categorical
atoms. Display text, title, opaque card ID, source path/hash, set identifiers,
family, style and split labels are absent from typed inputs. Unknown top-level
fields reject. Nonempty piloting/backside/leader, stateWatcher, private-program
or history-helper semantics require a separate reviewed contract and reject
here. They never turn into a neutral semantic vector. Empty programs do not
mean vanilla: ASH_048 has Shielded/Sentinel; LOF_151 has a root attachment
condition and upgrade bonuses even with no abilities or keywords.

Typed path keys × depth, ordered list indices, node/type/value symbols and two
numeric channels enter nonlinear leaves, followed by per-card mean pooling and
projection/LayerNorm to 16 dimensions. Both numeric scale and all native input
and target transforms use training cards/roots only. The reviewed categorical
schema includes all eight public definitions before labels. Held-only atoms
are allocated but the entire held bank is zero during training. New symbols
reject rather than mapping to an unknown category. Exact document and semantic
tensor hashes prove no collision among these eight inputs; they do not prove
injective learned embeddings or semantic quality.

The native ABI has 18 before-play global/source scalars, eight candidates with
34 public scalars each, and one int64 selected_candidate_indices per root.
Features expose source power/HP/damage/exhaustion with known flags, own aspects,
OTHER friendly space/Fighter counts, and candidate printed cost/uniqueness/
type/Vehicle/Fighter properties. The intended candidate is fixed by the plan
before play. No target offeredness, after-state, opponent private information,
hidden draw order or executable labels enter model inputs.

Card/context projections and candidate projections are 16d. Every source
head uses `[card,state,intended,card*state,card*intended]` → 32 → 9; candidate
heads use the same form with their own candidate → 32 → 7. Availability is
generally a source-only label, but the shared source head must learn invariance
to public intent. Including intent fixes the missing information needed for
event payment/cancellation and final location. It does not make the small
candidate panel a general action model.

## Native labels and frozen split

The unmodified runtime supplies 32 roots: eight complete source cards × four
fixed public states. Only actual offered play/menu/target actions are executed,
with a predeclared target and actual decline when that target is unoffered.
Unit entry/payment can precede an optional-trigger decline; declining never
means restoring the original root. Events have their native payment/target
boundary. Unrecognized/error/unsettled paths retain evidence and unknown masks.
Only the selected target receives measured future labels. Source heads occur
once per root, not eight times. Membership and outcome support stay distinct.

Six training cards are the first six above; LOF_155 and JTL_217 are held.
States 0–2 are training; state 3 is held: 18 training, 6 card-held, 6 state-held
and 2 joint roots. Alias, exact complete-definition and duplicate-program
groups must remain together. LOF_155 tests a new nonunique restriction within
the seen defeat/upgrade family. JTL_217 contains an entirely held exhaust
primitive: one card is an OOD stress case, not a sample of held effect families.
Report these held cards separately, each state and all negative heads. The
native card split and inherited deck-family split are different; LOF_155 occurs
in deck-training declarations, whereas JTL_217 is inherited deck-held-only.

Nine source heads cover availability/commitment, resources, arena/discard/hand
location, damage, Shield and exhaustion. Seven candidate heads cover observed
membership, removal and damage/exhaustion/Shield/power/HP deltas. All masks and
units are explicit. Per-card observed flags require successful paid, settled
native witnesses, not presence in a dataset. The endpoint table records all
declared/available/committed/settled roots and known coordinates, including
zero support. ASH_048 entry tests Shielded; Sentinel attack behavior is not
tested. No broad whole-card game conformance or synergy claim is available.

## Four matched card arms

Correct typed, ID-only, unused typed encoder + trained readout, and wrong typed
each receive seed 17, the same allocated graph, cloned initialization, 200
updates, batch size 18 with the same training-root schedule, AdamW .003 /
weight decay .0001 and preclip L2 limit 1. ID-only has six trained IDs and held
UNK0. Its typed encoder is frozen. The unused typed arm freezes the typed
encoder; correct/wrong freeze the unused identity table. Report allocated and
actually trainable counts rather than calling these counts identical.
The reviewed schema has 48 path, 7 type and 68 value symbols. Deterministic
parameter counts are 11,168 allocated in every arm; 11,056 trainable in correct/
wrong, 6,752 in ID-only and 6,640 in unused typed. The fitting process asserts
these counts against the instantiated graph before any forward.
Wrong inputs freshly draw another training card each update; fixed evaluation
swaps held cards and cycles training cards. Actual inputs/labels remain aligned.

The objective takes a mean within each root, then across observed roots, then
across training-varying native heads. Binary heads use BCE; signed continuous
heads use train-scaled smooth L1. Constant observed heads are retained with
auxiliary weight .1. No held statistics set scales or head weights. Save every
head's root support, labels, native values, MAE, signed residual, binary accuracy,
negative cases and all predictions. A native-label advantage over controls is
necessary evidence for this limited task; printed reconstruction is insufficient.
Binary metrics include positive/negative known-coordinate support, sensitivity,
specificity and balanced accuracy when both classes occur. Two zero-neural
baselines use literal all-zero decoded heads and training-only per-head means/
positive rates (fallback zero for unknown training heads). The zero-head
baseline is not a full unchanged physical state. A wholly unobserved training
head receives no supervised loss; its held result is not a trained-head binding
test and remains separate from training-constant-zero heads.

Exactly one all-32-root initialization batch and one final batch per arm yield
eight evaluation forwards. Final batches also return every 8×16 bank vector;
there are zero separate export forwards. Save final checkpoints, vector banks,
contracts, init/final predictions, schedules and all 800 durable update events.
No hyperparameter search, seed selection, joint fine-tuning or extra diagnostics.

## Frozen vectors into the actual decks

The later deck owner consumes `whole-card/fit-1/{correct-typed,untrained-typed,
id-only}-vectors.json` and the saved current-deck baseline, with all card weights
frozen. It uses all 251 identity rows with copy counts, shared printed metadata,
and separate admitted/represented/observed/trained/unknown masks. Missing
vectors have explicit unknown status, never vanilla status. Leaders/bases stay
separate from main-list copies. The same count-aware DeepSets φ/ρ graph, data,
initialization, targets, schedule and 100 updates per arm are required.

The inherited 22/5 deck-family split remains fixed. Thirteen printed composition
targets assess the plumbing and retained counts, not semantic transfer, strategy
or synergy. At 4.54% main-copy coverage this is a descriptive partial-coverage
comparison; no native-profile fitting or held-native-label reuse is added. An
encoder that mathematically permits interactions has not thereby learned them.
