# Public inputs and action binding

The native assay establishes two input-contract counterexamples. More model
capacity cannot distinguish identical consumed inputs. A corrected public
projector and explicitly bound rule interpreter predict every designated
surviving numeric endpoint exactly. This is a local causal diagnostic, not a
learned-transfer or competitive result.
The collisions concern the saved research effect-head contract, not every
encoder or the deployed competitive bot.

endpoints under one proposed action. The earlier branch task forecast the

Both defects concern available public information or its reference binding,

observation anchors. All **120 numeric coordinates and 24 removal zeros** agree

| Control | Damage (8) | Power (16) | HP (16) |

The strict legacy rule baseline abstains; its error is unknown, not zero. This
is conservative abstention rather than a claim that every legacy predictor must
abstain. Balanced equal-legacy-input pairs independently impose absolute-error floors
of **1 damage, 0.5 power and 1.5 HP** on their respective contrasts, without
fitting an estimator. The 72 recorded pairwise conflicts reuse these 16 roots;
they are not 72 independent examples.
The unbound controls receive richer repaired inputs, so the old-contract error
floors do not constrain their reported MAEs.

Four friendly-aspect flips, four opponent null pairs, four intended-parent swaps
and four creation/registration-order pairs pass. Separate pure tests rename all
fixture identities. Full definitions stay intact, including unscored keywords.
The two source programs were inspected before implementation; these are fresh
native observations outside the previous 12-source native assay, not a blind test of
general semantic interpretation. TRAIN is empty. Only the declared numeric
damage/stat endpoints are verified against native labels; no semantic
qualification is asserted and `semantic_validated` remains false. Exhaustion,
Shield and removal coordinates are constant zeros here, rather than additional
positively exercised mechanics. The controls are deliberately defective rule
interpreters, not learned architecture ablations.

## Repair and next architecture

[public_input_binding.py](public_input_binding.py) implements the corrected
before-action contract: public unit traits/aspects, owner-bound arena queries,
and `candidate_is_intended_host_or_target`. IDs resolve relations but do not
become numerical features. Unknown predicates or unsupported continuations
remain masked and stop the designated exact gate. Future attachment links and
action menus are label/legality evidence only.

Keep the shared 16-dimensional card encoder and fixed prediction widths for
the next learned comparison. Give the branch the intended-host relation and
typed, owner-bound public predicate summaries; retain the separate own-deck bag
and its knownness contract. Compare bound versus pooled resolution using the
same repaired information, initialization, widths, updates, masks and grouped
holdouts, with shuffled-label and fitted simple controls. First admit at least
two independent TRAIN program templates per scored mechanic/sign plus zeros.
That coverage gate is unmet here, so no neural fit or capacity increase ran.
Remaining-card history is still not wired into live inference.
