# Richer native observations did not rescue held-family transfer

The fixed-width experiment failed its predeclared behavioral gate. Rich typed
supervision nearly matched sparse supervision and beat the fitted identity,
untrained and wrong-typed controls, but lost to the training-mean and literal-zero
controls. Contrast direction accuracy also failed. This establishes a negative
result for this model, cohort and budget; it does not identify encoder capacity
as the cause. Broad `semantic_validated` remains false.

and some query/reference atoms were unseen in training. SEC_051's death/Experience
