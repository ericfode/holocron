# Effect identifiability versus capacity: independent design review

Status: conditional conceptual clearance for the next bounded experiment
after PR35, with exact native/learner source freeze still pending. This report
grants no execution approval. The reviewer has read saved sources/results only and has
run no native call, neural forward, optimizer, fit or metric replay.

The smallest useful contrast keeps the card encoder and predictor capacity
fixed and changes **training supervision on the same public roots**. It can
test whether richer identifiable observations help this model. It cannot by
itself establish that encoder capacity caused a remaining failure.

## Why the previous experiment cannot decide capacity

[The saved qualification report](ACTUAL_CARD_QUALIFICATION_RESULTS.md:39)
retains all sixteen heads: five target-delta heads had zero known training
coordinates, target removal had five positives and no negatives, and source
exhaustion had eight positives and no negatives. An unsupervised output row
moving through shared features or weight decay is not a learned effect test.
The trained encoder did move; lack of an optimizer path was not the observed
problem ([qualification evidence](ACTUAL_CARD_QUALIFICATION_RESULTS.md:75)).

The old held set mixes two different questions: LOF_155 is a held card within
the trained defeat/upgrade family with a new nonunique restriction; JTL_217 is
one wholly held exhaust primitive. The latter is not evidence of learned
exhaust-family transfer ([split scope](ACTUAL_CARD_QUALIFICATION_RESULTS.md:9)).
The eight cards cover 65/1,432 actual main copies. No new result here qualifies
the other 107 runtime-admitted identities or the whole actual corpus.

The saved outcome comparison remains visible: correct-typed card-held macro
MAE .33094 versus ID .16135, unused typed .14104, shuffled typed .10872 and the
training-only constant .30631. These are saved values, not fresh calculations.
Its narrow target-membership balanced-accuracy gain does not repair native
outcome regression. The deck-stage printed-composition readout is a separate
question and cannot establish semantic transfer or synergy.

## Paired learner and masking requirements

Rich and sparse runs must share documents, public input tensors, train/held
partition, initial state, allocated and trainable parameter counts, optimizer,
update count, minibatches, wrong-card schedule and final evaluation roots. Only
the predeclared **training mask** changes. A sparse missing label is mask0,
never a fabricated observed zero. Neither arm may train on held outcomes.

Freeze numeric/input scales and target centers from training roots only. Use a
common rich-training scale, primary head list and fixed loss coefficients for
both arms. Declare that the sparse arm receives this common training-derived
support metadata. PR35 derives varying-versus-constant loss ownership and the
head denominator from support ([scalers](whole_card_encoder.py:236),
[loss](whole_card_encoder.py:298)); independently recomputing these for each
mask would additionally change head weighting. That bundled change would need
its own interpretation. Both runs must use the same full known evaluation
mask and same primary head list; masked-only success is not a comparison.

Keep the PR35 16d representation, path/token caps, nonlinear leaf/card/head
widths and public ABI fixed. If new public symbols require predeclared table
rows, state their exact capacity delta and keep it equal between paired runs.
Do not describe a vocabulary resize as an exact 11,168-parameter replication.
Known held definitions may allocate public categorical symbols before labels
if disclosed; held symbols and outcome-derived scales must not receive held
training gradient or fit statistics. Save the complete cloned initialization.

Preserve the ID-only, unused-typed, shuffled-typed and train-only constant/zero
comparisons without implying their trainable capacity matches the typed arm.
Within each corresponding control, the rich/sparse pair must still match.
Support tables, signed native errors and sensitivity/specificity/balanced
accuracy belong beside aggregate MAE. Classes or heads without support remain
null, not perfect. Keep the saved PR35 errors as the historical baseline.

## What the outcomes would support

The subsequent root [hypothesis](EFFECT_IDENTIFIABILITY_HYPOTHESIS.md) and
[learner protocol](EFFECT_IDENTIFIABILITY_LEARNER_PROTOCOL.md) fix a positive
gate before labels: rich typed must have at least 10% lower primary
family-first identifiable target-effect error than the strongest eligible
sparse/ID/unused/wrong/training-prior control. Primary effect heads need at
least four TRAIN observations including observed zero and nonzero. Correlated
power/HP errors count as one statistic group; at least two held families and
three held program groups must have support. Source cost/location and offered
membership remain secondary.

The ten fixed contrast pairs are JTL_102 states0↔1 and2↔3; each of LAW_127 and
ASH_228 states0↔1 and2↔3; and LAW_131↔JTL_079 within all four states. Direction
uses a ±.25-native-unit deadband and must score at least .75 family-first;
zero native contrast requires predicted absolute contrast≤.25. Contrast error
must also improve at least 10% versus the strongest eligible control, with at
least four observed pairs from two families. Unknown pairs remain listed and
unknown; measured signs cannot select the panel. These choices are a frozen
new test, not a replay of untouched research evidence. Actual source and
label-support checks still gate execution and interpretation.

* Rich improves over sparse at fixed capacity on the same adequately supported
  held families: the supervision intervention helped this model/cohort. This
  does not prove broad semantic transfer or that capacity never matters.
* Rich fits its training contrasts but fails held identities/states: transfer
  remains unqualified. Representation, capacity, optimization and sample size
  remain possible explanations; a larger encoder is not established as the
  remedy by this contrast.
* Rich cannot fit identifiable training contrasts: report that failure and
  the optimization/accounting checks. It is not an isolated capacity finding.
* Positive/no-change support or determining public features are missing:
  the experiment cannot decide the proposed question. Stop at that scope.

No gameplay, strength, synergy or full-runtime semantic claim follows from
this bounded native prediction assay. Previously inspected cards and states
must be disclosed; the new held set is not wholly untouched research evidence.

Read-only inspection of the pinned complete rows adds four specific gates:

* `SEC_051` has both When Played enemy-area −3/−3 and an enemy-unit-death
  event hook that gives Experience. The full document contains executable
  `dataPath.argumentBinding.id/bindAt/readAt`; the old tokenizer rejects the

  `argumentBinding`: source-position callback names become deterministic

  `bindAt`, `readAt`, root and ordered steps. This is acceptable if the

  A field-name deletion or global string replacement is insufficient.
* `ASH_171` includes nested `ifYouDo.title`; `JTL_102` includes a dynamic
  `contextTitle` display-expression array as well as its executable
