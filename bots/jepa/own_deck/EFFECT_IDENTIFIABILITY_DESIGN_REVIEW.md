## Why the previous experiment cannot decide capacity

[The saved qualification report](ACTUAL_CARD_QUALIFICATION_RESULTS.md:39)
retains all sixteen heads: five target-delta heads had zero known training
coordinates, target removal had five positives and no negatives, and source
exhaustion had eight positives and no negatives. An unsupervised output row
moving through shared features or weight decay is not a learned effect test.
The trained encoder did move; lack of an optimizer path was not the observed
problem ([qualification evidence](ACTUAL_CARD_QUALIFICATION_RESULTS.md:75)).

The old held set mixes two different questions: LOF_155 is a held card within
the trained defeat/upgrade family with a new nonunique restriction; JTL_217 is
one wholly held exhaust primitive. The latter is not evidence of learned
exhaust-family transfer ([split scope](ACTUAL_CARD_QUALIFICATION_RESULTS.md:9)).
The eight cards cover 65/1,432 actual main copies. No new result here qualifies
the other 107 runtime-admitted identities or the whole actual corpus.

The saved outcome comparison remains visible: correct-typed card-held macro
MAE .33094 versus ID .16135, unused typed .14104, shuffled typed .10872 and the
training-only constant .30631. These are saved values, not fresh calculations.
Its narrow target-membership balanced-accuracy gain does not repair native
outcome regression. The deck-stage printed-composition readout is a separate
question and cannot establish semantic transfer or synergy.
