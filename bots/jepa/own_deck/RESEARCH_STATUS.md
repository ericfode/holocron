The completed native experiment used existing whole-card runtime admission,
actual source/menu/target actions and settled native outcomes. Collection source
is `aa2526b84c5705dcbf12a7d043df29d57d41c2f2`; fit source is
`fb8853c8a52d0ceed4d7c4221fbef008ccd2e747`. It has 100 training roots,
20 held-program roots, 40 held-state roots and eight joint roots. The two held
programs target base versus unit domains, so there are no held-program-only
same-candidate committed-delta contrasts. Joint target outcomes cover five roots
and three states. Source availability is counted once per root; expanded
candidate coordinates are correlated. No game or competitive outcome was
measured by this experiment.

The small architecture carries a 16-dimensional shared typed-program vector
into separate source and candidate heads. Ordered path/value tokens retain
quantities, selector roles, costs and sequences; active-token mean pooling and
normalization avoid the previous fixed-padding amplitude difference. Each
candidate head sees only that candidate's public attributes plus two globals.
Source-self queries are a separate 0.1 auxiliary and never enter other queries.
The four arms share initialization, minibatches, update counts and train-only
scaling. [Contracts, weights and raw evidence](grounded-contrast-evidence/index.json)
include the saved 12 program vectors, with no additional export forward.

For actual deck-aware decisions, retain the opt-in original/remaining two-token
design described in [README](README.md): pool canonical card-copy counts through
one shared card encoder, then pass those tokens to the actor, action scorer and
leaf judge under a common objective. Original composition and its curve can be
known from the declaration. Remaining composition stays explicitly unknown
until a branch-local observation ledger supports it. Public revealed cards may
inform that ledger; hidden draw order and opponent private cards may not.
The current standalone action experiment does not train that deck-conditioned
policy or establish synergy. Existing competitive artifacts remain unchanged.
