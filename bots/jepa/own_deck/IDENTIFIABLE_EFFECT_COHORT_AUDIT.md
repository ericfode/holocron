The proposed 12-card cohort has stronger effect contrasts, but its deck-copy domain is smaller: **58 / 1,432 copies (4.0503%)**, versus 65 / 1,432 (4.5391%) in the historical eight-card bank. This audit uses public typed definitions and declared counts only: no native/model execution or new training labels. Proposed domain coverage is not serialized, native-observed, learned-vector or qualified coverage.

The fixed cohort is seven training cards—ASH_048, LOF_151, SEC_142, JTL_144, JTL_217, ASH_171, SEC_051—and five held cards—JTL_102, LAW_131, JTL_079, LAW_127, ASH_228. Static frontend checks distinguish loader admission from model representation. The unchanged old frontend accepts 99 / 115 admitted identities (526 / 635 copies); 16 / 109 copies reject, all on unreviewed identity/display/binding fields. Ten proposed cards pass unchanged (46 copies). Recursive display-only cleanup admits ASH_171 (+3 copies). Invocation/read timing and event root/steps remain intact. Dropping the binding relation or feeding its source-position ID would be wrong. Contract-v3 calls the actual new learner’s pure `canonical_document` and frozen tokenizer: all 12 documents are unique and need at most 123 tokens / path depth 15, under the unchanged 512 / 32 limits. This is frontend verification only, with zero neural encoding. Pin the final learner source separately before execution.

SEC_051’s event-hook XP program stays represented, but native fixtures avoid all opponent deaths, so that hook is explicitly unmeasured. SEC_142’s onAttack registration also remains outside an entry-only test. Neither full definitions nor a settled entry witness establishes whole-card behavior. No mechanic field, ability side or ordered operation sequence may be dropped to simplify a fixture.

Alias canonicalization uses the pinned catalog’s setId/setCodes/setCode, never numeric source IDs. There are no ambiguous aliases or exact normalized machine-program groups crossing the proposed train/held split. LAW_131/JTL_079 share a numeric-template family and are jointly held; LAW_127/ASH_228 are jointly held near-clones despite their distinct in-play guard. ASH_048 shares a program/keyword signature with out-of-cohort LAW_118: any later extension must carry that group together. Printed stats are separate inputs, so this program signature does not assert whole-card equality.

All new cards need observable positive and negative/no-change consequences in training and held fixtures, not merely legal-use/resource labels. A zero is valid only from actual paid/settled before/after observations of the same public anchor. Unavailable, cancelled, unsupported, pending or unobserved targets remain unknown. Defeated-target counters cannot be imputed from IR. The native rich and sparse variants must use identical roots/evaluation labels; sparse TRAIN masks are fixed from treatment/input or old-mask logic before outcomes.

Source IDs, paths/hashes, titles, family IDs, completion labels, offeredness, after-state fields and target labels stay out of feature tokens. Context/target features must contain every legitimate predicate dependency before the action and use common treatments across cards. For example, own-hand count is absent from the old 18-field context, so JTL_153 was not selected. Literal/cost/target interventions should vary public state within each family, rather than allowing context setup or source printed cost to stand in for the effect. Static full-cohort vocabulary review is transductive syntax availability, not native-outcome exposure or unseen-schema qualification.

The old checkpoint has only two current training identities (ASH_048, JTL_217; 22 copies) and **no current held identities** in its measured bank. A matched old-model new-held baseline is unsupported; any frozen compatible-head diagnostic must disclose its vocabulary/ABI/input and measured-identity limits. Old/new latent spaces cannot be mixed. The potential domain union is 18 identities / 101 copies (7.0531%), but at this prelaunch snapshot the new 12 have no new native/model evidence; do not label that union observed or qualified.
