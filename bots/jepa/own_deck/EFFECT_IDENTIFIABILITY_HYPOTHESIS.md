# Identifiable native-effect supervision after PR35

Hypothesis: PR35's sparse effect observations are sufficient to explain much of
its failed held-card transfer. Five target-delta heads had no training labels;
target removal had no observed negatives. Parameter movement and composition
decoding did not establish semantic learning.

The smallest new intervention keeps the 16-dimensional encoder and leaf/head
widths fixed. Rich and sparse arms use the same new graph, initialization,
training roots, update schedule, TRAIN-only transforms and held evaluation
labels. Sparse loss masks hide the five formerly unobserved target-delta heads;
they do not turn missing outcomes into zero. ID-only, untrained-typed and wrong-
typed controls receive the same new data/update budget. New public categorical
symbols and card cardinality can change table counts, which must be disclosed;
they cannot differ between rich and sparse arms.

The actual-corpus cohort is frozen before native labels:

- TRAIN: ASH_048, LOF_151, SEC_142, JTL_144, JTL_217, ASH_171, SEC_051.
- HELD: JTL_102 (count-based damage), LAW_131/JTL_079 (targeted stat debuffs),
  LAW_127/ASH_228 (attached-unit exhaustion).
- Four public worlds per card, three TRAIN and one state-held, yield 48 roots.
  Equivalent attached-exhaust and targeted-debuff variants stay together.
  All held primitives occur in different TRAIN templates. Three held template
  families are a small diagnostic, not a population-level strength estimate.

Every future label comes from an actual settled native endpoint on one declared
public target or observation anchor. Actual target commitment, attached host
selection and an area-effect observation anchor are distinct fields. Unsettled,
dead/missing or inapplicable numeric states remain unknown. All input-hash/label
conflicts must be reported. No hidden draw order or opponent private identity is
an input. Full definitions remain intact; surviving SEC_051 fixtures leave its
death/Experience hook unmeasured. Display cleanup and scoped callback alpha-
normalization preserve executable fields and are reviewed separately.

Before fitting, all four planned mechanics require at least two observed zero
and two nonzero TRAIN roots per head. Held support must cover two families and
three program groups; the fixed contrast panel needs four measured pairs in two
families. Missing support stops the fit and remains a collection result.

The predeclared primary outcome is family-first held error on identifiable
target consequences. Source cost/location and membership are secondary. A positive gate requires at least
10% lower primary error than the strongest eligible sparse/ID/untrained/wrong,
training-mean or literal-zero control, together with the protocol's fixed
direction/contrast checks and all
per-family errors. Untrained primitives, correlated HP/power coordinates and
single-family exceptions cannot be counted as independent successes. No nearest-
neighbor or printed-composition result can pass this gate.

The old checkpoint is immutable. Only ASH_048 and JTL_217 overlap this cohort,
both in new TRAIN; no new HELD card overlaps. Its unchanged old results and any
exact-compatible context diagnostic must be reported separately, without
resizing/remapping old weights or claiming a full matched held-family baseline.

Rich failure would show that this data expansion did not rescue this encoder;
it would not establish capacity as the cause. No width search, held-driven
tuning, joint fine-tuning, opponent games or deployment belongs to this round.

$0. Exact guarded stage quotas, source hashes, split/support gates and the
