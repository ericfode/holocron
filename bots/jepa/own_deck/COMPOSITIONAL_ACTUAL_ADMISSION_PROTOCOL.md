Publish the synthetic result first. Audit the exact unchanged candidate before
allocating any native or learned evaluation. The catalogue and runtime-admission
snapshot are pinned inputs, not a claim of present engine conformance.

Pass every complete catalogue row, unchanged, to `encode_program(row)`. Preserve
its full-row canonical hash, runtime membership and exact first rejection. Do
not crop an attractive effect, remove rules, add implicit selectors or extend
the numeric domain. The parser internally reads abilities; even parser success
would require a separate full-card and public-state review before collection.
If any row unexpectedly passes, fail this frozen zero-acceptance boundary test
and stop; there is no downstream native or inference allocation here.

Reconstruct the result independently for every row without importing the tested
parser. Report catalogue, runtime-admitted and exact-parser counts separately.
Runtime admission does not establish semantic conformance. Family freshness is
semantic, not a new card ID: all 32 representable owner/arena/cap/operator/amount
tuples already have exposed native outcomes in the preceding experiment. Pin
that original plan, prove its complete factorial coverage, and verify three
saved committed cases per program in the recovered native receipt. No supported
semantic family is fresh under this contract. No labels or predictions are
needed to establish this input boundary.

Two ordered stages, audit and independent verification, each reserve ten wall
seconds, one CPU worker and at most 1 GiB sampled RSS. Every native application,
neural forward, backward, optimizer update, numerical fit, paid job and dollar
allocation is zero. Initialize one new append-only ledger after committing and
pinning the source. Preserve the previous closed ledgers, failed reservations,
807 charged native applications, 266.6158477078425 charged seconds, unknown
historical observed count, $100 ceiling and $25 reserve. No production changes.

An admission failure is an untested-transfer result, not a measured prediction
failure. Any broader grammar, numerical OOD adapter, actual-family dataset or
training is a distinct experiment requiring its own source and budget freeze.
